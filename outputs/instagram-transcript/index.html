WHAT WORKS HERE

Clear about the limits.

Public videos with spoken audio

This Instagram transcript generator accepts public Reel links, video post links, and share links that resolve to a public video. Private posts, Stories, photo-only posts, and videos Instagram restricts are not supported.

Original-language transcription

Language availability depends on the transcription provider and the video. Translation and speaker labels are not included.

One video at a time

Availability depends on the transcription service and remaining service capacity. Existing captions are used when available; otherwise speech is transcribed with AI. Restricted videos may not be accessible. Share-link compatibility has not yet been verified.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you press play again.

How do I turn Instagram Reels into text?

Copy the public Reel link, paste it above, and select Get transcript. Keep this tab open while it processes. You can copy the result or download it as TXT, SRT, or VTT.

Can I convert an Instagram video to text without captions?

The provider can transcribe spoken audio when existing captions are unavailable. Music-only or silent videos may have no speech to transcribe.

What is included in the subtitle downloads?

SRT and VTT contain the recognized speech with segment start and end times. TXT contains the transcript without timecodes. All three downloads come from the same transcription.

How accurate is the transcript?

Accuracy depends on the language, background noise, pronunciation, and recording quality. No accuracy percentage is promised. Check the transcript against the original audio before publishing.

Why might a public link not work?

Instagram may restrict access, remove a post, or require a login to retrieve it. A public link is not a guarantee that its audio is accessible. Try the original Reel or video post link. This tool does not ask for your Instagram password.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No account is required to use this page. Service limits may temporarily prevent new requests.

Is my transcript saved?

This page does not save transcript history. Processing and retention by the provider follow its policies. The result stays in this tab until you clear it or reload the page. Download anything you want to keep.
