# Instagram Transcript

Turn a public Instagram Reel or video post into text in your browser. Paste the public link on the [Instagram Transcript tool](https://reeltranscriptlab.com/), select **Get transcript**, and keep the tab open while the transcription provider processes it.

## Input and output

The page accepts public Reel links, video post links, and share links that resolve to a public video. It handles one video at a time. The result is spoken-word text that can be copied or downloaded as TXT, SRT, or VTT. Subtitle downloads include segment start and end times.

Private posts, Stories, photo-only posts, and videos the provider cannot retrieve are not supported. Language availability depends on the provider and video. Translation and speaker labels are not included. No accuracy percentage is promised; review the transcript against the source audio.

The public guide lookup is a separate, read-only service. It searches and returns published guides; it does not submit transcription jobs, fetch arbitrary URLs, or require login. See the [agent access documentation](https://reeltranscriptlab.com/ai/) and [public guide index](https://reeltranscriptlab.com/guides/).
